# 📜 Code of Conduct

**Customer Segmentation & Intelligence Platform**

---

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Code of Conduct is to ensure a **collaborative, respectful, inclusive, and professional environment** for everyone involved in this project.

This repository focuses on advanced topics such as **customer analytics, machine learning, causal inference, embeddings, and real-time systems**. Constructive collaboration and thoughtful communication are essential to maintaining high technical standards and ethical responsibility.

We are committed to providing a **harassment-free, safe, and welcoming experience** for all contributors, users, and maintainers, regardless of background or level of experience.

---

## 2. Expected Standards of Behaviour

All participants in this project are expected to uphold the following standards:

### ✔ Professionalism & Respect

* Use welcoming, inclusive, and respectful language
* Be patient, considerate, and constructive
* Disagree thoughtfully and focus on ideas, not individuals
* Assume good intent and communicate clearly

### ✔ Constructive Collaboration

* Provide clear, actionable, and respectful feedback
* Support and mentor newcomers where possible
* Document changes and decisions transparently
* Help maintain high standards of code quality and reproducibility

### ✔ Responsible & Ethical AI Practice

* Avoid misuse or harmful applications of models
* Consider fairness, bias, ethical impact, and societal implications
* Do not embed sensitive or personally identifiable information in datasets or models

### ✔ Scientific & Technical Integrity

* Do not fabricate, exaggerate, or misrepresent results
* Clearly document assumptions, limitations, and modelling choices
* Strive for reproducibility and transparency in analysis

### ✔ Privacy & Confidentiality

* Do not upload real customer data or identifiable information
* Use anonymised, aggregated, or synthetic datasets only
* Respect confidentiality in discussions and private correspondence

---

## 3. Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are **not tolerated** under any circumstances:

### ❌ Harassment or Abuse

* Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks
* Any form of bullying or hostile behaviour

### ❌ Discrimination or Exclusion

* Discriminatory language or actions based on gender, race, ethnicity, religion, age, disability, sexual orientation, nationality, or any protected characteristic

### ❌ Toxic or Disruptive Communication

* Insults, demeaning comments, or mocking language
* Aggressive or hostile tone
* Repeatedly derailing or disrupting discussions

### ❌ Unethical or Irresponsible Technical Behaviour

* Intentionally misleading analysis or results
* Manipulating models or metrics to deceive
* Uploading sensitive, proprietary, or illegal data
* Using this repository to build harmful or unethical ML systems

### ❌ Malicious Code or Sabotage

* Introducing security vulnerabilities intentionally
* Breaking functionality without justification
* Deleting or altering files maliciously

---

## 4. Responsibilities of Maintainers

Project maintainers are responsible for:

* Monitoring interactions and contributions
* Reviewing and responding to reports of misconduct
* Editing, rejecting, or removing content that violates this Code of Conduct
* Taking appropriate action against repeat or severe violations

Maintainers may **temporarily or permanently restrict participation** to protect the community.
They are **not obligated to provide detailed justification** when removing harmful content.

---

## 5. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project-related spaces, including but not limited to:

* The GitHub repository
* Issues and pull requests
* Discussions and comments
* Community or collaboration channels
* Private communications related to project work

---

## 7. Enforcement

If a violation is confirmed, maintainers may take one or more of the following actions:

* Issuing a warning
* Requesting clarification or an apology
* Removing comments, commits, or pull requests
* Temporarily or permanently banning contributors
* Escalating the issue to GitHub if required

Actions will be taken **fairly, consistently, and proportionally** to the severity of the violation.
